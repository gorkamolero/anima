# SHADOW OVER INNSMOUTH — Opening Video Scripts

## "Hermes Enters the Book"

**Core premise:** Failure leads him. The blood was always calling. His life falling apart made him finally answer.

---

# OPTION A: "THE BOOKSHOP"
*Quiet failure. He's hiding from life in stories.*

## SCENE 1 — USED BOOKSHOP, LATE AFTERNOON
- Hermes browses alone. Rain on windows.
- His phone buzzes. He ignores it.
- **HERMES (V.O.):** *"Seventeen missed calls. I'll deal with it tomorrow."*

## SCENE 2 — THE BOOK
- A book falls from a high shelf. Lands at his feet.
- No title on the spine. Leather. Old.
- He opens it. The pages are blank.
- Then words appear: *"You found this because you were meant to."*

## SCENE 3 — THE PULL
- The bookshop goes dark. Wind from nowhere.
- The pages flip themselves.
- Words crawl across the paper: *"The bus to Innsmouth departs in five minutes."*
- He tries to close the book. It won't close.

## SCENE 4 — THE FALL
- The page becomes a void.
- He falls in.
- BLACK.

## SCENE 5 — BUS STATION
- Eyes open. Bench. Book in his hands.
- Two destinations on the board: BOSTON. INNSMOUTH.
- **HERMES (V.O.):** *"...Okay."*

**Emotional Arc:** Avoidance → Curiosity → Compulsion → Fall → Acceptance

---

# OPTION B: "THE INHERITANCE"
*Family failure. Something was left for him. He wasn't supposed to open it.*

## SCENE 1 — APARTMENT, NIGHT
- Boxes everywhere. Someone died.
- Hermes sits with a whiskey. Staring at a locked wooden box.
- **NOTE:** *"Don't open this. — Dad"*
- He opens it.

## SCENE 2 — THE BOOK
- Inside: a book. Bound in something that isn't leather.
- Symbols on the cover that hurt to look at.
- He opens it. The first page: a family tree. His name at the bottom.
- Below his name: *"Blood calls to blood."*

## SCENE 3 — THE READING
- He reads aloud. The words taste wrong in his mouth.
- The apartment walls start dripping. Salt water.
- The lights flicker.
- The book SPEAKS: *"You're late. We've been waiting."*

## SCENE 4 — THE DROWNING
- Water rises from the pages. Fills the room.
- He can't escape.
- He goes under.

## SCENE 5 — BUS STATION
- Gasping awake on a bench.
- Dry. Book in his hands.
- **HERMES (V.O.):** *"That wasn't a dream."*

**Emotional Arc:** Grief → Defiance → Discovery → Drowning → Awakening

---

# OPTION C: "THE LAST RESORT"
*Personal failure. He's at rock bottom. The book is an exit — or an entrance.*

## SCENE 1 — BRIDGE, NIGHT
- Hermes stands at a railing. Looking down.
- His phone rings. He throws it into the water.
- Behind him: a homeless man. Watching.

## SCENE 2 — THE OFFER
- **HOMELESS MAN:** *"You looking for a way out?"*
- He holds out a book. Old. Wrong.
- **HERMES:** *"What is it?"*
- **HOMELESS MAN:** *"A door. Some people find what they need on the other side."*
- **HOMELESS MAN:** *"Some people don't come back."*
- **HERMES:** *"...Good."*

## SCENE 3 — THE READING
- He opens the book. The first line: *"There is a town that doesn't exist."*
- He keeps reading.
- The bridge disappears around him. The water rises up.
- He doesn't flinch.

## SCENE 4 — THE PASSAGE
- He's pulled into the pages.
- Not drowning. Falling.
- Through ink. Through words.

## SCENE 5 — BUS STATION
- Sitting on a bench. Alive.
- The book is in his lap. It says: *"Welcome to Innsmouth."*
- **HERMES (V.O.):** *"I wanted out. I didn't say which direction."*

**Emotional Arc:** Despair → Encounter → Surrender → Passage → Unexpected Life

## KEY MOMENTS

**THE KNIFE (Shot 9-10):**
- First line: *"You don't have to go back."*
- His face: relief, then fear, then relief again.
- He knows it's a trap. He chooses it anyway.

**THE ARC:**
1. **Haunted** — dreams of something he doesn't understand
2. **Collapsing** — life falls apart around him
3. **Offered** — the book presents escape
4. **Accepts** — relief, fear, relief
5. **Arrives** — new world, same soul

**THE THESIS:**
The blood was always calling. His failure made him finally answer.
