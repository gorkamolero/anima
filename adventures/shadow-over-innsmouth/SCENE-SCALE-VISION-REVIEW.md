## Rule

Do not accept a scene-scale pass from numeric continuity alone. The pass is:

1. capture full-size per-frame review images from the running game,
2. inspect them with vision,
3. write findings,
4. adjust free-walk limits, route-only samples, ground-line points, or scale
   factors,
5. regenerate the frames.

The current exterior result uses `freeWalkBounds` plus `freeWalkRanges` to keep
normal clicking in `x:200..760` and `x:1280..1520`. The clipped left edge,
Kat-overlap span, and right-side bus/door positions are diagnostic or authored
route samples only.
